They changed their lives to live their dream.

Marie and Renaud de Goumoëns left their former life to build treehouses in the trees of their estate in Ogens, crossed by a river, and to spend more time as a family.

Each stay carries the name of one of their children — Camille, Mathis, Alanis and Mila — and has its own world. Welcome, and get ready to dream.

To the table, by basket

Meals are taken in your cabin or on its terrace — there is no common dining room. We gladly adapt the baskets for allergies, intolerances or vegetarian meals.

The fondue basket

A fondue kit for a typically Swiss evening: cheese from the St-Cierges dairy, bread, sides and dessert. To enjoy on the terrace (not in the cabin).

Included

Breakfast

Warm bread, jam, Baba's honey, seasonal fruit, fruit juice, dairy-fresh yoghurt, muesli and a piece of Gruyère. Everything to start the day to birdsong.

The terroir basket

Seasonal regional produce: cheeses and cured meats, fresh eggs, crunchy vegetables (a soup in the cooler season), good bread and a dessert — to enjoy in the cabin or on the terrace.

Renaud’s cellar

Renaud has selected white, rosé and red wines he loves, plus craft beers he will happily introduce you to. Fruit juices, lemonades, and self-service snacks or ice creams for sweet tooths.

Picnics allowed, but no cooking on site. For safety reasons, barbecuing is forbidden on the grounds.
